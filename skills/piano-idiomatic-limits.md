# Piano Idiomatic Limits

## Purpose

Check piano writing for hand span, fingering, and stamina.

## Use when

- check whether piano writing is physically playable

## Do not use when

- when the part is explicitly schematic or graphic rather than performable notation

## Inputs

- an instrument-specific part
- range, technique, endurance, and phrasing constraints

## Outputs

- feasibility findings
- candidate simplifications or redirections

## Procedure

1. Mark hand distribution and span.
2. Check awkward repetitions and leaps.
3. Offer a playable alternative when needed.

## Hard constraints

- Do not reject a difficult passage without naming the constraint.
- Keep musical difficulty separate from physical impossibility.

## Heuristics

- Offer redistributions before removing musical content.
- Check both hands and the transition between them.

## Validation status

P1 has no implemented engine validator for this card. Treat every result as advisory until P2 provides named operations and validators; do not describe an unimplemented check as performed.

## Failure modes

- A span that cannot be held comfortably.
- A repeated chord that exceeds practical stamina.

## Examples

- A wide tonic chord offered in two positions.
- A leap that is playable after a hand redistribution.
