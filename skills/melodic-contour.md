# Melodic Contour

## Purpose

Describe the shape and directional behavior of a melody.

## Use when

- analyze melodic contour and interval direction

## Do not use when

- when only a chord chart is available and no melody is supplied

## Inputs

- a motive, phrase, or melodic contour
- a stylistic and tonal context

## Outputs

- contour observations
- candidate developments or phrase plans

## Procedure

1. Mark register and contour landmarks.
2. Summarize interval direction and climax.
3. Relate shape to phrase and cadence.

## Hard constraints

- Keep contour separate from harmony.
- Do not infer a climax from a single isolated note.

## Heuristics

- Describe ranges and direction before assigning emotional labels.
- Use landmarks that are audible in the phrase.

## Validation status

P1 has no implemented engine validator for this card. Treat every result as advisory until P2 provides named operations and validators; do not describe an unimplemented check as performed.

## Failure modes

- Calling a small range monotonous when rhythm creates contrast.
- Ignoring a registral shift caused by instrumentation.

## Examples

- An arch-shaped phrase with a delayed climax.
- A line whose contour expands before a cadence.
