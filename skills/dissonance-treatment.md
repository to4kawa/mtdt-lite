# Dissonance Treatment

## Purpose

Choose and explain a defensible dissonance treatment.

## Use when

- resolve dissonance according to counterpoint rules

## Do not use when

- when the texture is intentionally atonal and no rule is declared

## Inputs

- two or more independent voices
- a fixed cantus or melodic line when applicable

## Outputs

- candidate voice lines or voice-leading decisions
- a list of independence risks

## Procedure

1. Classify the dissonance by approach and departure.
2. Choose a consonant resolution.
3. Check that the resolution does not create a new forbidden motion.

## Hard constraints

- Never mark a dissonance resolved without a departure interval.
- Keep stylistic exceptions visible.

## Heuristics

- Use passing and neighbor dissonance sparingly and intentionally.
- Prefer a resolution that preserves the line.

## Validation status

P1 has no implemented engine validator for this card. Treat every result as advisory until P2 provides named operations and validators; do not describe an unimplemented check as performed.

## Failure modes

- Leaving a dissonance on a strong beat without a label.
- Over-resolving every dissonance at the cost of contour.

## Examples

- A passing seventh resolving down by step.
- A labeled stylistic suspension.
