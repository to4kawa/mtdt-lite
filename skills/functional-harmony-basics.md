# Functional Harmony Basics

## Purpose

Analyze a progression with key, scale, and cadence evidence.

## Use when

- analyze functional harmony and Roman numerals

## Do not use when

- when the style is intentionally nonfunctional

## Inputs

- chord tones or a harmonic progression
- key, meter, and phrase context

## Outputs

- Roman-numeral or function analysis
- candidate chord and cadence choices

## Procedure

1. Establish the local key.
2. Identify chord quality and function.
3. Label ambiguity and modulation rather than forcing one answer.

## Hard constraints

- Keep Roman numerals tied to the stated analytical convention.
- Do not equate a chord label with a complete voice-leading solution.

## Heuristics

- Use cadential and bass evidence to choose among labels.
- Keep analysis descriptive before proposing a rewrite.

## Validation status

P1 has no implemented engine validator for this card. Treat every result as advisory until P2 provides named operations and validators; do not describe an unimplemented check as performed.

## Failure modes

- Using one symbol system inconsistently.
- Ignoring chromatic chords or tonicization.

## Examples

- A I-V-vi-IV loop.
- A tonicization followed by a return to the global key.
