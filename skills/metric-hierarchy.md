# Metric Hierarchy

## Purpose

Describe the shared metric vocabulary used by rhythm and form skills.

## Use when

- explain meter beat hierarchy and measure structure

## Do not use when

- when the meter is not established

## Inputs

- pitch, interval, key, or meter context
- a notation or audio-derived description when available

## Outputs

- normalized musical vocabulary
- a concise explanation of relationships

## Procedure

1. Identify the meter type.
2. Mark beat units and subdivisions.
3. Relate measures to the phrase or pulse hierarchy.

## Hard constraints

- Keep vocabulary separate from applied rhythm techniques.
- Do not call a pulse a beat without evidence.

## Heuristics

- Use consistent terms across simple, compound, and asymmetric meters.
- Show the hierarchy before applying transformations.

## Validation status

P1 has no implemented engine validator for this card. Treat every result as advisory until P2 provides named operations and validators; do not describe an unimplemented check as performed.

## Failure modes

- Confusing beat hierarchy with a visual beaming choice.
- Assuming all measures carry equal musical weight.

## Examples

- 3/4 as three quarter-note beats.
- A 5/8 grouping with two plus three eighth notes.
